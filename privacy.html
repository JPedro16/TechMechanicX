Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how TechMechanicX, an engineering consultancy agency providing freelance and contract engineers, collects, uses, shares and protects your personal data.

Last updated: 1 June 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how TechMechanicX — an engineering consultancy agency providing freelance and contract engineers — collects, uses, and discloses information when you use our website or engage our services. It explains your privacy rights and how the law protects you.

By using our website or engaging our services, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

Who We Are

TechMechanicX is an engineering consultancy agency based in the Netherlands that provides freelance and contract engineers to clients across the Netherlands and Europe. Our engineers work on site or remotely and deliver mechanical design, automation, and structural engineering services.

For the purposes of the GDPR, TechMechanicX is the data controller and is responsible for the processing of your personal data.

  • Email: HR@techmechanicx.com
  • Website: www.techmechanicx.com

What Personal Data Do We Collect?

We collect and store:

  • Contact and enquiry details — your name, business email address, telephone number, company name, company address and the content of your enquiry, when you make an enquiry with us or contact us for support purposes.
  • Engagement and project details — when you engage our engineers, the details required to plan and deliver the work, including engagement agreements, timesheets, billing and invoicing data, and any project-related information you share with us.
  • Engineer details — when you ask us to propose one of our engineers, the professional background, competence and availability information you provide, so that we can present a suitable profile.
  • Technical and usage data — limited technical information such as your IP address, browser type, operating system, device type and the pages you visit on our website, collected automatically to keep the site secure and to improve it.

We do not intentionally collect special categories of personal data (such as health, political opinions, or trade union membership) through this website. If your project requires us to process such data, we will agree a specific arrangement with you in writing first.

We process personal data only for the purposes listed below, and only on the legal basis stated.

Who Do We Share Your Personal Data With?

We share personal data only where it is necessary for the purposes set out above, or where the law requires it:

  • Our engineers and freelancers — where they need access to the data required for the project they are assigned to. They are bound by confidentiality obligations and by the security requirements of your own organisation.
  • Service providers — third parties who provide services connected to this website or to our business activities, but only to the extent necessary to provide those services, such as IT providers who host, develop and support our website and cloud environment, and payment providers who process invoices.
  • Our clients and project counterparties — where you ask us to propose a specific engineer, we will share the professional background and competence information you provided with that client so they can assess the candidate.
  • Professional advisers and auditors — where we are required or permitted to disclose information to obtain legal, tax or accounting advice.
  • Public authorities — when required by law or in response to a valid request from a court, government agency or supervisory authority.
  • A buyer or potential future buyer of our business — in the event of a merger, restructuring, sale of assets or transfer of the business, subject to appropriate confidentiality safeguards.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Where Do We Transfer Your Personal Data?

Your personal data is processed at our operating offices in the Netherlands and, where we engage a service provider, in the places where that provider operates. This means that some data may be transferred to, and maintained on, servers located outside the European Union or the European Economic Area.

Where a recipient is located in a country with a lower level of data protection than within the EU/EEA, we use the European Commission's standard contractual clauses, together with a transfer impact assessment and supplementary measures where necessary, to ensure an adequate level of protection for your personal data.

How Long Do We Keep Your Personal Data?

We store personal data for as long as necessary to fulfil the purpose for which it was collected, and in accordance with our internal record retention schedule. This means we delete your personal data when it is no longer necessary to process a request, to deliver a project, or to manage our relationship with you.

  • Enquiry and contact data is kept for as long as needed to handle the enquiry and the resulting business relationship, and for a reasonable period afterwards to answer follow-up questions.
  • Engagement, timesheet, invoicing and accounting records are kept for the statutory retention period applicable in the Netherlands, generally 7 years, because of our tax and financial reporting obligations.
  • Project data and engineering deliverables are kept for the duration of the engagement and for the period agreed with you in the engagement terms, after which they are returned or deleted at your instruction.
  • Usage statistics which have been anonymised may be kept for longer.

How Do We Protect Your Personal Data?

The security of your personal data is important to us. We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures, including:

  • Encryption of data in transit and at rest, and secure remote access to client systems where our engineers work directly on your environment.
  • Access control on a need-to-know basis, with unique accounts, strong authentication and periodic review of access rights.
  • Confidentiality obligations for all personnel involved, reinforced by a non-disclosure agreement, which is standard in every collaboration.
  • Regular backing up and restoration testing of systems holding personal data.

No method of transmission over the internet, and no method of electronic storage, is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your personal data, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

Cookies

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies. Cookies are small files placed on your device that allow a website to store details of your browsing activity, and which are used to make our site work correctly and to understand how it is used.

We use the following categories of cookies:

  • Strictly necessary cookies — required for the website to function and for basic security. These cannot be switched off.
  • Statistics cookies — used to understand how visitors use the website so that we can improve it. These are only placed if you give consent.

You can instruct your browser to refuse all cookies or to indicate when a cookie is being sent. If you do not accept cookies, some parts of the website may not function. You can manage or withdraw consent for statistics cookies at any time through your browser settings.

Your Rights

In relation to the personal data that we hold about you, you have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you (Art. 15).
  • Correct or erase your personal data (Art. 16 and 17), although we may not be able to process your request or continue the engagement if we need the data to deliver the agreed services.
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interest, and restrict how we process your data (Art. 18 and 21).
  • Receive your data used to provide you with information you requested, to deliver a project, or to manage our relationship, in a machine-readable format that you can transfer to another controller (Art. 20).
  • Withdraw your consent at any time, including for marketing communications (Art. 7(3)).

We may not accept a request to erase your personal data where we are required to comply with a legal obligation, or where the data is needed in relation to a legal claim.

How to exercise your rights

Requests to exercise your rights should be addressed to us by email at HR@techmechanicx.com. We will respond within one month of receiving your request. If you are not satisfied with our response, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority.

Complaint Authority

If you have a complaint regarding our processing of your personal data, you are entitled to report this to the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens (Dutch Data Protection Authority):

https://autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl/

You may also have the right to lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority in the country where you live, work, or believe an infringement has occurred, and to bring proceedings before a court in accordance with Article 79 of the GDPR.
